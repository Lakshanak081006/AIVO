# Demonstration Guide

1. Sign in with `demo@example.com` / `Demo@12345`.
2. Open **New Trip**.
3. Use: `Plan a two-day trip from Coimbatore to Chennai for two people next weekend under ₹20000. I prefer train travel and museums.`
4. Show extracted requirements, agent tasks, transport and hotel comparisons.
5. Open the itinerary and budget charts.
6. Open the action log to explain agent decisions.
7. Use **Demo Mode** and trigger heavy rain or transport cancellation.
8. Compare the regenerated plan and cost.
9. Approve and perform the simulated booking.
10. Submit feedback and approve a suggested preference.
